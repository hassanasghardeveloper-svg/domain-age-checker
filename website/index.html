FAQ

Common questions.

Is DomainAge really free?

Yes — completely free, no signup, no ads, no tracking, no upsells. Data comes from free public APIs (RDAP and Cloudflare DNS).

Does it track my browsing?

No. DomainAge only sends the bare domain (not the full URL or your identity) to rdap.org and cloudflare-dns.com. Results are cached locally for 7 days. There are no analytics and no servers of our own.

Why isn't this on the Chrome Web Store?

It's coming. For now, install via "Load unpacked" using the steps above — it works exactly the same.

Does the toolbar badge slow down my browser?

No. The badge is fetched from a 7-day local cache after the first visit, with deduplicated requests. The first lookup adds ~500ms in a background thread.

Some country-code domains don't work?

RDAP (the modern WHOIS replacement) covers most generic TLDs and major country codes, but not every ccTLD has joined yet. Domains without RDAP data show a "not found" message.

Will it work on Firefox?

Not yet. DomainAge is built for Chromium-based browsers (Chrome, Edge, Brave, Arc, Opera, Vivaldi). A Firefox build is on the roadmap.
